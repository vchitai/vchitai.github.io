  • grpcx

    2026

    • Own project
    • Active
    Who it’s for
    Backend teams standing up new gRPC and REST services.
    What was in the way
    Every new service re-implemented the same plumbing — server lifecycle, structured errors, i18n, auth, observability — slightly differently, so how reliable a service was depended on who happened to write it.
    What I did

    A team can start a service with the reliability primitives already in place, instead of bolting them on after the first production incident.

  • One business rule, four different answers

    2024

    • Sanitized production case
    • Complete
    Who it’s for
    An operations team whose daily work depended on generated documents agreeing with each other.
    What was in the way
    A configuration-dependent business rule was applied correctly in one path but silently dropped from other generated outputs, so the same request could produce different answers depending on where you looked.
    What I did
    Traced the rule through calculation, persistence, preview and export, named the invariant all four paths were supposed to share, and added regression coverage that compares outputs against each other.
    What became possible

  • Quantum Computing Race 2026

    2026

    • Own project
    • Complete
    Who it’s for
    Technical leaders and investors comparing fast-moving quantum-computing platforms without relying on raw qubit counts or vendor roadmaps alone.
    What was in the way
    Superconducting, trapped-ion, neutral-atom, photonic, topological, and annealing systems publish metrics that are not directly comparable, while roadmap claims are often presented beside demonstrated results.
    What I did
    Built an evidence-synthesis report that separates physical scale, fidelity, logical error correction, access, product maturity, and vendor targets; each material claim is tied to a cited source and caveated by evidence level.
    What became possible
    Readers can compare who leads on each dimension and distinguish demonstrated capability from announced roadmap instead of forcing one misleading overall ranking.
